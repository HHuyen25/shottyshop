Our Story

Founded in 2020, SHOTTYSHOP started as a small passion project by a group of K-POP fans who wanted to bring authentic Korean merchandise to Vietnam. Today, we have grown into one of Southeast Asia's most trusted K-POP retail platforms, serving thousands of fans across the region.

We work directly with official distributors in Korea to ensure 100% authentic products, including albums that count towards HANTEO and GAON charts. Every album purchased from SHOTTYSHOP helps support your favorite artists directly.
